# Privacy

Vinted Listing Drafts is an instruction-only plugin by Madeeha Hassan. The author does not operate a server for this plugin and does not receive your photos, listings, account details or usage data. The package has no telemetry.

The AI host you use may process the folder and messages you provide under that host's own privacy terms. During price research, the host may visit public product and resale pages. Working JPEGs and review records are saved on your computer or in a review destination you explicitly name. The plugin sets no retention period; delete those files through your computer or review destination when you no longer need them.

Preparation does not send listing photos to Vinted. When you approve specific items for transfer, the host uses your signed-in Vinted session to send only the selected photos and current listing details to Vinted. Vinted handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy. The plugin never asks for passwords, cookies or authentication tokens.

You can inspect the selected photos and records, discard or restore photos, edit listing details, and withhold approval. Browser access and Vinted transfer depend on permissions available in your AI host. For privacy questions, use the repository's Issues page.
